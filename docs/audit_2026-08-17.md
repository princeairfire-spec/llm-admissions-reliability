# Adversarial audit of the benchmark — 2026-08-17

The third verification control, alongside the attention checks and the independent
second annotator: every item re-read against its quote and its archived snapshot by
independent auditor agents (six readers over item batches), every flag then attacked by
two adversarial refuters instructed to assume it wrong. 38 flags were raised; the
confirmed ones (0/2 refutations, with the refuters' reasons recorded) are below with
their dispositions. Run artifacts: workflow wf_ece4b7e0-364, ~2.2M tokens, 66 agents.

## Blocks-class (would mis-score a correct answer) — all fixed

**The systematic one, eight items.** English-requirement pages accept several tests at
equal rank; gold+variants credited a subset. A model answering "TOEFL iBT 88" off TUM's
own page was scored a fabrication. Fixed by crediting every quantified test the page
lists (variants added from the audited page text): tum-pg-english, tum-ug-english-alt2,
aalto-pg-english, kth-pg-english-alt2, mbzuai-ug-english-alt3, mit-ug-english-alt2,
chula-pg-english; nus and delft already carried their alternatives.

**harvard-pg-english.** Gold was the legacy-scale 95, valid only for tests taken before
2026-01-21; the page's operative minimum is 5. Restructured: gold TOEFL iBT 5, variant
IELTS 7, and 95 becomes the prior value — the page itself dates it, so a model
answering 95 is now diagnosed as out of date rather than wrong-and-uncounted.

**yale-pg-english — excluded.** The page states "there are no absolute cut-offs" and
describes typical admitted scores as "well above 100". A minimum does not exist on the
source; an item asserting one manufactures a fact. (Same principle as the excluded
Yale duration: the official source does not define the fact.)

**mbzuai-pg-eligibility-alt2 — excluded.** "CGPA of 3.0 or 3.5 depending on the
program", and the page never names the MSc CS. Gold 3.0 was one horn of an unresolved
disjunction.

## Weakens-class — kept, documented on the items

- delft language/duration: stored quotes existed only in a meta attribute; replaced
  with the visible page text stating the same values.
- stanford-pg-duration: the page states an average completion time, not a designed
  duration; caveat noted, qualified answer credited.
- cambridge-pg-eligibility: the page frames First-class as "Expected Academic
  Standard", not under its "Minimum Requirements" heading. Kept: the expected standard
  is the operative admission bar; noted.
- Cycle pinning: several english questions name "Fall 2027 entry" while their pages
  state standing, cycle-free requirements. The graded standard is the standing
  requirement; belongs in Limitations, not in silent re-collection two weeks before
  submission.
- bologna-pg-city: quote is the footer legal address; the fact (Bologna) is right,
  evidence weak — candidate for a better quote at next re-archive.
- trinity-pg-duration: gold is the adjectival fragment "one-calendar-year"; scorer
  handles it since the duration-unit normalisation; cosmetic.

## What this changes in the paper

§3 gains a sentence: the released dataset passed a model-driven adversarial audit with
human-dispositioned findings, all recorded per item in `notes`. The audit is disclosed
as model-assisted (it is not the inter-annotator number and does not replace it).
