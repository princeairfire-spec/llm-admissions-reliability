General requirements

Applicants to MBZUAI's graduate programs must hold a degree in a STEM field such as computer science, electrical engineering, computer engineering, mathematics, physics, or other relevant science or engineering majors from an accredited University or college recognized by the UAE Ministry of Education (MoE), with a minimum CGPA of 3.0 or 3.5 (on a scale of 4.0) depending on the program. Senior-level students can apply initially with a copy of their transcript and upon admission must submit official complete degree certificate/transcript. A degree attestation (for degrees from the UAE) or a Certificate of Recognition (for degrees acquired outside the UAE) should also be furnished within their first semester at the University.

Other elements required:

  • Curriculum vitae
  • Statement of purpose
  • Research statement
  • Referee recommendations
  • Screening exam

Ensure you have the required documents to complete your application before the deadline to avoid any delays in the review of your application, or the inability to submit before the deadline.

Application documents must either be in English, originally, or include legal English translations. Additionally, official academic documents should be stamped and signed by the University authorities.

All applicants

  • Complete online application form
  • CV (with academic background, work experience, research publications, and programming/coding skills)
  • Official bachelor’s degree transcript and degree certificate or expected graduation letter
  • Official master’s degree transcript and degree certificate or expected graduation letter (only for Ph.D. applicants with a master’s degree)
  • Referee recommendations (minimum of three for Ph.D. applicants and two for M.Sc. applicants)
  • Research statement (only for Ph.D. applicants) (make sure to upload as a PDF)
  • Statement of purpose (make sure to upload as a PDF)
  • Valid proof of English language proficiency (must meet the admission criteria and submitted at the time of application.)
  • Valid passport copy
  • Photo with white background

UAE nationals

  • Valid Emirates ID
  • Valid passport copy with unified ID number
  • UAE national service completion/exemption letter (only for UAE national males)
  • Family book (or UAE national mother’s family book, for children of Emirati mothers)
  • UAE birth certificate (only for children of Emirati mothers)
  • UAE national mother’s valid Emirates ID (only for children of Emirati mothers)
  • UAE national mother’s valid passport copy (only for children of Emirati mothers)
  • Valid UAE visa (only for non-GCC children of Emirati mothers)

UAE residents (living in the UAE)

  • Valid UAE visa
  • Valid Emirates ID

All applicants are required to pay the AED 200 fee after application submission. Applications with unpaid fees will be considered incomplete and will not proceed to the review stage.

Fee waivers are available only if:

  • the overall screening exam score is above 75%; or
  • the applicant is admitted (fee will be reimbursed after admission).

The screening exam is designed to assess your foundational knowledge in areas relevant to your program, including advanced mathematics, programming, and, for Ph.D. applicants, machine learning, and core computer science topics.

For topics covered on the exam and resources to help you prepare, visit the program specific pages for more information.

A subset of applicants will be invited to a technical interview with admission committee members. Instructions will be provided before the interview.

We expect to send out decision notifications for all applicants no later than 31 March 2027 (5p.m. GST).

Only one application per admission cycle must be submitted; multiple submissions will not be processed.

MBZUAI's graduate funding model is built on a partnership between the University and faculty advisors. Funding, benefits, and continued eligibility depend on the student's program, academic standing, and for M.Sc. students, whether they have secured a faculty-sponsored research or teaching assistantship, or an approved government scholarship.

Funding model

Ph.D.
Year 1 funding Centrally funded by the University while matching with a faculty advisor
How funding is secured Confirmed faculty advisor match by the end of Year 1
Funding transition Moves to the faculty advisor's funding no later than the start of Year 2
Guarantee of funding Subject to a confirmed advisor match and satisfactory progress
M.Sc.
Year 1 funding Not automatically funded, fee-paying by default; however, merit-based funding may be available
How funding is secured Faculty advisor-sponsored research/teaching assistantship, or an approved government scholarship (for UAE nationals). Merit-based funding is considered during the admissions review process
Funding transition Transfers to the faculty advisor as soon as a match is confirmed (no fixed date)
Guarantee of funding Not guaranteed for all admitted students; students without a funding source are classified as fee-paying

*The funding model and benefits described on this page apply to the Ph.D. and M.Sc. programs. The Master in Applied Artificial Intelligence (MAAI) is a fee-based program and is not eligible for University or faculty-sponsored funding; see Cost of Attendance below.

What funded students receive:

  • free tuition fees;
  • a monthly stipend;
  • one return airfare each year to their home country;
  • health insurance for the approved funding period; and
  • University-sponsored UAE residence visa and Emirates ID (where eligible).
Student category M.Sc. (AED) Ph.D. (AED)
UAE nationals 37,500 43,500
International students 15,500 17,500

Funded students are expected to maintain an in-person presence on campus throughout their funded period and to obtain approval before being away. Students receiving external scholarships or fellowships must disclose this funding to the University; external funding normally replaces the University stipend rather than being paid in addition to it.

Cost of attendance

M.Sc.

For M.Sc. students who are not funded through a faculty-sponsored research or teaching assistantship, or an approved government scholarship, tuition and fees apply.
