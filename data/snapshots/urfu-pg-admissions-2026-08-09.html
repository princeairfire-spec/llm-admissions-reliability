Admission & Arrival 2027

All Information About Admission 2026-27

Application

  • Check out UrFU entry requirements:
Bachelor's Degree Programs

Eligibility Criteria

  • Previous education: completed course of secondary education (colleges and vocational / technical schools)
  • Entrance exam: two tests in major subjects and mandatory* Russian language test (the entry level of Russian language is B1-B2). There might be an additional creative exam (e.g. drawing/ drafting/ composition etc.) for certain specialties.

*If an applicant has a certificate of completion of a Russian language course (either from UrFU Russian Language School or any other authorized institution), it can be submitted instead of taking the language test.

Required Documents

  1. Scan of the applicant’s passport
  2. Translation of the applicant’s passport into the Russian language (with notarial attestation)
  3. Previous education documents (school diploma / certificate or equal) with notarized translation into the Russian language.
  4. Full transcript of grades with notarized translation into the Russian language.

Note: please consider that in accordance with the Russian education standards all education documents issued in other countries require legalization / apostille verification, otherwise they cannot be considered by the Admissions Office.

Deadlines

  • Application period: June 20 to August 20

Learn about UrFU Bachelor's programs

Master's Degree Programs

Eligibility Criteria

  • Previous education: Completed Bachelor's / Specialist's degree program
  • Entrance exam: Online interview with the Program Administrator
  • Required level of language of instruction (Russian / English): B1-B2

Required Documents

  1. Scan of the applicant’s passport
  2. Translation of the applicant’s passport into the Russian language (with notarial attestation)
  3. Previous education documents (Bachelor’s diploma or equal) with notarized translation into the Russian language.
  4. Full transcript of grades (for the whole period of studies) with notarized translation into the Russian language.

Note: please consider that in accordance with the Russian education standards all education documents issued in other countries require legalization / apostille verification, otherwise they cannot be considered by the Admissions Office.

Notarized translations of the documents into the Russian language are absolutely required regardless of the language of instruction of the target program.

Deadlines

  • Application period: June 20 to August 20

UrFU Preparatory Department

Eligibility Criteria

The Preparatory Department (Russian Language School) is open for everyone who finds interest in studying the Russian language, no restrictions in terms of the age (except that the student should be over 17) or previous education level apply. In case you want to continue the studies at the University after completion of the language course you have to make sure that your education is in accordance with the admission criteria of the program.

Contacts:

  • for admission write to m.e.kopyrin@urfu.ru 
  • on academic issues – rfl@urfu.ru

Apply to the Russian Language School of UrFU

Required Documents

  • Scan of the applicant’s passport (should be valid for at least 2 years from the day you apply);
  • Translation of the applicant’s passport into the Russian language (done in a translation agency);
  • Filled in visa application form;
  • Bank statement showing funds sufficient (at least) for covering the tuition fee for the 1st semester of studies (in case you are not paying 100% of the tuition fee) 
  • For more information please visit the RFL School Website

Deadlines

  1. Foundation year program: 1,5 year (Russian language + specialty subjects) – online application form (till March 16, 2026)
  2. Russian as a Foreign Language program

The deadline for the tuition payment under the contract is August 21, 2026.

Arrival

  • Buy tickets and inform us about your arrival (date, border crossing point) via arrival form 10 days before arrival
  • Before arriving to Russia, foreign students must inform the university by e-mail at least 10 days prior to the date of entry into the Russian Federation and by filling out the arrival form
  • After sending the letter, you will receive information about the entry procedure by e-mail.

Important! For those who has come to Russia after December 29, 2021: under the Federal Law No. 274-FZ of 01.07.2021 "On Amendments to the Federal Law “On the Legal Status of Foreign Citizens in the Russian Federation" and the Federal Law "On State Fingerprint Registration in the Russian Federation", all foreign citizens entering the Russian Federation are subject to mandatory medical examinations (first of all) and dactyloscopic registration within 90 calendar days upon arrival.
