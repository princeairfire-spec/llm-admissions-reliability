Admission Timeline

A. General Application Timelines

  • KAIST International Graduate Admission
    • Spring Admission: Mid August – Early September
    • Fall Admission (Early Track): Early December – Late December
    • Fall Admission (Regular Track): Early March – Late March
  • Global Korea Scholarship (GKS)
    • University Track: Early February
    • Embassy Track: vary by the Korean Embassy in your country of citizenship

B. Application Dates for the 2027 Entries

  • KAIST International Graduate Admission
    • Spring 2027 Entry: August 18 – September 1, 2026
    • Fall 2027 Entry : TBA
  • 2027 Global Korea Scholarship (GKS)
    • University Track: to be announced in February
    • Embassy Track*: to be announced in February
      • refer to the official website of the Korean Embassy in your country of citizenship
      • GKS scholars are asked to take a Korean language course for one year and must obtain level 3 or above of TOPIK.
