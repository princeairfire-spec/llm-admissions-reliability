Going to university is an investment in your future. You'll need to consider the cost of your tuition and your living (or ‘maintenance’) costs.

These amounts can seem daunting. But there's lots of support available to help you finance your studies.

Tuition fees

  • Tuition fee information for Home students
  • International student fees and costs

Not sure what your fee status is (Home or international/overseas)? Check the Tuition fee status information (Cambridge Students website) for guidance.

Living costs

Living costs cover whatever you need to pay for, excluding tuition fees, while you're at university. For example, accommodation, food, study costs, personal expenses and transport.

Living costs at Cambridge

Are you an international student? You should also check the international fees and costs page.

Financial support

There are various sources of financial support available to help towards the costs at Cambridge. This includes government loans, University support and College awards.

  • Cambridge scholarships, bursaries and financial support
  • International financial support
  • UK student finance (government support)
