Tuition Fees for Students

Chulalongkorn University has established tuition fees and related charges according to the Announcement on Tuition Fee Rates B.E. 2562 (2019), covering tuition fees for Thai and international students, lecturers, application fees, late payment penalties, and academic document service fees. These fees are collected based on the level of study and faculty group to ensure clarity and uniformity throughout the university.

The tuition fee for international students in Thailand is quite reasonable compared to the long-term skills and knowledge that a student will be equipped with when they graduate. Tuition fees for international students are divided into five groups

Fee rates are collected according to the group of faculties.

Tuition fees for students in the Life Sciences Group 1
  • Faculty of Dentistry
  • Faculty of Medicine
  • Faculty of Veterinary Science
  • Multidisciplinary Program in Bioinformatics and Computational Biology
  • Multidisciplinary Program in Biology Medical Microbiology
  • Multidisciplinary Program in Biomedical Sciences
  • Multidisciplinary Program in Dental Biomaterials Science
  • Interdisciplinary Program in Physiology

Remarks

  • Books: Approximately 5,000 (150)/semester
